Services

The Telecom Services We Provide

Complete field and technical delivery for mobile network sites, from installation to handover.

Microwave Link Installation and Alignment

Supply, installation and alignment of microwave links between sites, with signal tuning for the best link performance. We specialize most in Huawei links.

Radio Equipment (RBS) Installation

Installation and connection of radio units and antennas on towers and ground sites according to the site design and the LLD.

Commissioning and Operation

Bringing sites on air and testing them (Integration & Commissioning) and making sure they enter service without faults.

Rollout and Modernization Works

Delivering new Rollout sites and network modernization and expansion works (Swap / Modernization) for mobile operators.

Provisional and Final Acceptance

Preparing sites for PAC acceptance tests and closing all remarks until final acceptance (FAC) is obtained.

We can carry out all types of field actions on sites, whether Rollout or Radio Expansion, on Huawei or Ericsson equipment.

How We Work

How a Site Moves Through Our Work

A clear delivery cycle from receiving the site to final handover.

Site Receipt and Review

Reviewing the site files and the LLD, and assigning the responsible engineer and coordinator and the execution plan.

Field Installation

Installing links, radio and Baseband equipment and site connections according to the design.

Commissioning and Testing

Bringing the site on air and testing its performance before acceptance.

Acceptance and Handover

Preparing the site for PAC, closing any remarks, and following up until final acceptance (FAC).
